User manual

⤓ Download Word

Step by step, one task at a time. Settings is under the Settings tab of your account (the page you land on after signing in).

Set up the company

  1. Open Company.
  2. Fill in the name, legal name, address, phone and email. These details appear on your proposals.
  3. Add the logo (optional) and save.

Add a contact (client or supplier)

  1. Open Contacts and choose New contact.
  2. Type the name, the person, the email and the phone. Mark what it is: client, supplier, general contractor.
  3. For a supplier, mark the cost codes it supplies (for example, siding, trim, insulation). The quote list of a project is split by supplier using these marks.
  4. Save. The contact is now available in every project.

Invite a user

  1. Open Users and choose Invite.
  2. Type the email and the name, and choose the access role (see Roles and permissions).
  3. The person receives an email with a link, sets a password and signs in. The invitation expires after a few days; you can send it again.
  4. To remove access, deactivate the user. Nothing they did is deleted.

Adjust what each role can do

  1. Open Access roles and pick a role.
  2. Check or uncheck the permissions. For example, an estimator can measure but not change the catalog and not see costs.
  3. Save. The change applies the next time each person opens the system.

My profile

Open My profile to change your name, language, email or password.

If something goes wrong

Symptom Cause What to do
A person cannot see Measure Takeoff Their role does not have the permission to measure. Open Access roles and turn on Measure.
A person does not see prices Dollar amounts need a separate permission. Turn on the catalog or cost visibility permission for the role.
The invitation email did not arrive Wrong address or spam folder. Check the address and the spam folder, then send the invitation again.
