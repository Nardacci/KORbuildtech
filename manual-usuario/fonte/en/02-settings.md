# Settings: the company and the people

**Settings** is where the administrator organizes the company. Every registration screen follows the same design: a create button at the top, **search**, a **grid** with the rows and a **side panel** to create and edit. To leave the panel, use **Cancel**, the **×**, the **Esc** key, or click outside it.

## Users

Here are the people who sign in to the system and each person's role. Only the administrator registers and changes them.

![Users](img:usuarios)

### Register a person

1. Tap **New user**.
2. Enter the **name**, the **email** (it is the login), the **phone**, the **job title** and the **access role**.
3. Tap **Create user**.

![New user](img:novo-usuario)

The system creates the login and shows the **temporary password just once**. Use **Copy address, email and password** and send it to the person through a secure channel.

![Temporary password](img:senha-provisoria)

> **Careful:** the temporary password does not appear again. If it gets lost, open the person with **Edit** and use **Generate a new temporary password**. The previous one stops working.

If the email already has a KORbuild account (in another company), the system tells you. In that case use **Invite by link**: the person receives a link and joins your company with the login they already have.

### Edit a person

Tap **Edit** on the person's row to change name, phone, job title, role and status. To change only the role, use the selector on the row itself. A deactivated person loses access, and their history stays.

![Edit user](img:editar-usuario)

Two rules protect the company: nobody changes their own access, and the company always keeps at least one active administrator.

## Access roles

Roles define what each person can do. The screen lists the roles, how many permissions each one has and how many people use it.

![Access roles](img:perfis)

Tap **Edit** to see the permissions. They appear as switches, grouped by area. The **Admin** role is locked: it cannot be changed or deleted.

![Role editor](img:perfil-editor)

To create another role, tap **New role**: start blank or from an existing role and adjust. A role in use cannot be deleted, and the screen says who uses it.

![New role](img:novo-perfil)

## Contacts

The directory of clients, contractors, suppliers and subcontractors. Each contact has **tags** (client, supplier…), and the chips above the grid filter by them.

![Contacts](img:contatos)

Tap **New contact** or **Edit**. The panel keeps the person's and the company's data, the address and notes. A contact needs at least one tag.

![Contact](img:contato-painel)

> Register the client's contact before you create your first project in Takeoff: the project's client is chosen from there.

## Job roles

Each job role (Carpenter, Painter…) has an **hourly rate**. Takeoff uses it for labor pricing: a labor item linked to a role takes that role's rate, and you do not type a price on the item.

![Job roles](img:funcoes)

Open a role to see the **hourly rate history**. Each rate applies **from a date** and never erases the previous one: a proposal uses the rate that applied on the day it was issued. To change it, tap **New hourly rate** and enter the rate, the date and the reason.

![Job role and hourly rate history](img:funcao-painel)

## Company

The company's data: name, legal name, EIN, address, phone and email. Only someone allowed to manage the account (the administrator) changes it; for everyone else the screen is read-only.

![Company](img:empresa)

## My profile

Your personal data: name and phone, language, sign-in email, password and which notices also arrive by email. Only the administrator changes your access role and permissions.

![My profile](img:meu-perfil)
