# Getting started

## Sign in

Open the KORbuild address, type your **email** and **password** and tap **Sign in**. Check **Keep me signed in** if the device is yours alone.

![Sign-in screen](img:login)

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password**: we email you a link.

## Create your company's account

If your company does not use KORbuild yet, tap **Create your company's account** and enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.

![Create the account](img:criar-conta)

After signing in for the first time, the **Your company** screen asks for the company name and **how the company works** (general contractor or subcontractor). Whoever creates the company becomes the **Admin**.

![Register the company](img:sua-empresa)

> **If the administrator already registered you:** do not create an account. Sign in with the email and temporary password they gave you (see "First sign-in with a temporary password" below).

## Home

When you sign in, **Home** shows the essentials, from top to bottom:

- **Number strip:** four cards that open the matching list: **Open proposals**, **Won this year**, **Open projects** and **Late** (in red, with the project and the days late).
- **Needs you now:** a thin line with what is pending (for example, a daily report waiting for your review). Each item opens the right screen. With nothing pending, it says "All caught up here".
- **Planned vs done:** the line chart of the project that needs the most attention (the latest one). The dashed line is what should be done along the schedule; the orange dot is what is done today.
- **Project progress:** the list beside it, with each project's bar of done against planned. **Click a row to switch the chart's project**; click the name to open the Gantt.
- **More indicators:** whatever is left (daily report, money in play, projects by status, quotes, catalog and prices…) stays collapsed and only appears when there is something to show.

![Home](img:inicio)

## First sign-in with a temporary password

When the administrator registers you, they get a temporary password (three groups of four characters, like `Ab3d-Ef5g-Hj7k`) and pass it on to you. On your first sign-in:

1. Sign in with your email and the temporary password.
2. The system takes you straight to the **new password** screen. Nothing else opens until you choose yours.
3. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and confirm it. Done: the temporary password stops working and only you know yours.

## Download this manual

After signing in, open your account menu (your initials) and tap **User manual (PDF)**. The file comes in the language you picked in the system (Portuguese or English).

## Sign out

Open your account menu (your initials, top right) and tap **Sign out**. The session ends on this device even if the internet is unstable.
