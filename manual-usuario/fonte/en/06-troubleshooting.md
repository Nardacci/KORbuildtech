# Questions and common problems

**I cannot sign in.** Check the email and password. If you forgot it, use **Forgot password**. If the administrator just registered you, use the exact temporary password they gave you, and choose a new password when the system asks.

**The temporary password is gone.** Ask the administrator: under **Settings › Users › Edit**, they use **Generate a new temporary password**.

**I do not see a menu or button.** What appears depends on your access role. Ask the administrator to check the permissions under **Access roles**.

**"This email already has an account."** The person already has a KORbuild login, in another company. Use **Invite by link** instead of creating the user directly.

**The project does not appear in Daily.** Only projects released to the job appear. In Takeoff, open the project's **Overview** and check that it is **Released for construction**.

**The day's report does not open / there is no "New daily report".** The report is due from the **Construction start**. Check the date on the project's **Overview**.

**The "Release for construction" button is disabled.** The **Job** card says what is missing: plans, checked scale, who does each task, measuring and schedule.

**The measurement looks wrong.** Check the sheet's **scale** against a known dimension. Without a checked scale, measuring is not reliable.

**The app shows an old version.** Close the app and open it again. The update bar appears when the new version is downloaded.
