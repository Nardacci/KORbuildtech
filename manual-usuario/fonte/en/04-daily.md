# Daily: the daily construction report

**Daily** keeps the **daily report** (DDR). The foreman fills it in on the phone, on site; the administrator or the manager reviews it and **approves and seals** it. Only projects that were **released to the job** appear in Daily.

## On the phone: filling in the day's report (foreman)

On signing in, the foreman lands on **Today**, with their jobs.

![Daily: Today](img:daily-hoje)

Tap the job. The page shows the **current phase** (the schedule task in progress today) and the **New daily report** button.

![Daily: the project](img:daily-projeto)

The report has sections, and each gets a ✓ when it is ready: **Weather**, **Crew**, **Work performed** and **Photos**.

![New daily report](img:daily-rdo)

1. **Weather:** the app marks the morning and afternoon weather from the job's location; adjust if needed.
2. **Crew:** add who worked and the equipment used.
3. **Work performed:** describe what was done. You can dictate with the microphone.
4. **Schedule task:** on each activity, choose the schedule task it belongs to, and enter the **% done of the task** and the **hours spent** on it today. This is what updates the Gantt after approval.
5. **Photos:** attach the day's photos (optional).

![Activity linked to the schedule task](img:daily-atividade)

When everything is ready, tap **Submit for approval**. If there are no photos, the app asks and you can **Submit without photos**.

![Submit for approval](img:daily-enviar)

![Report submitted](img:daily-enviado)

> **No internet?** The report stays on the device and is sent when the signal returns. The indicator at the top shows how many reports are ready to send.

## On the computer: approving the report (administrator or manager)

On Daily's **Dashboard**, submitted reports appear under **Awaiting approval** (and under **Approvals** in the menu).

![Daily: dashboard](img:daily-painel)

Tap **Review** to read the full report. You can **Approve and seal** it or **Request changes from the field** (the foreman corrects and submits again).

![Review the report](img:daily-revisar)

On approval the report is **sealed**: it gets a verification code (like `A1B2-C3D4-E5F6`) and can no longer be changed. The system tells you the schedule received the progress.

![Report approved and sealed](img:daily-aprovado)

## Progress on the Gantt

The % done and the hours entered in the approved report go to the project's schedule. On the **Gantt**, each task shows its % and late tasks appear marked.

![Gantt with the report's progress](img:gantt)

**Home** also summarizes the projects: progress, deadlines and what is late.

![Home with projects under way](img:inicio-final)
