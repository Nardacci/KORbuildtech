# Bids: invite, quote and award

**Bids** is part of Takeoff. Here a company **invites others to quote a service** (the contractor) and the invited company **answers with its proposal** (the subcontractor). The same screen serves both sides: the menu has a single item, **Bids**, with two tabs: **Sent** (the ones you sent out) and **Received** (the ones sent to you).

In the examples, **Ridgeway Construction** (contractor) invites **Northfield Builders** and **Bayside Interiors** to quote "Framing and drywall" for the *Riverside Office Building* job.

> Whoever sends and awards needs the **Run bids and award** permission; whoever answers needs **Respond to invitations and submit bids** (see the roles in the Settings chapter).

## Send a bid (contractor)

In **Bids › Sent**, tap **New bid**.

![Bids: sent, no bids yet](img:bids-vazio)

In Takeoff, a bid starts from a **project**: choose the project (the subcontractors' tasks, the plans and the scope come from it).

![New bid: choose the project](img:bids-projeto)

The form has four tabs: **Service and scope** (the job, the service, the stages to quote and what is included), **Documents** (the drawings as PDF), **Dates and requirements** and **Invitees**. Invitees come from your **Contacts**: anyone with a KORbuild account gets the invitation inside the system; anyone without one gets a **link** by email.

![New bid](img:bids-nova)

Save the draft whenever you like. When you tap **Send invitations**, the bid becomes **open** and the invitations go out. For invitees who receive a link, the **Copy the link** button appears on the page itself.

![Bid sent](img:bids-enviada)

The **Sent** tab lists all of them, with the status of each.

![Bids: sent](img:bids-lista)

## Receive and quote (subcontractor)

Whoever was invited sees the invitation in **Bids › Received** (and in the bell).

![Bids: received](img:bids-recebidas)

Open the invitation: you see the contractor, the job, the scope, the deadlines and the drawings. Choose **I'll bid** or **Decline** (with the reason).

![Invitation received](img:bids-convite)

When you tap **I'll bid**, the system creates a **project** of yours with the contractor's drawings already split into sheets. Measure, build the schedule and **issue the proposal** as in any project. On the proposal page, **Submit through the platform** sends the proposal to the contractor, with the execution time.

![Send the proposal through the platform](img:bids-proposta)

Questions about the scope? Write in the bid's **Questions** tab. The contractor answers only you or **everyone** (without saying who asked) and can notify everyone with an **addendum**.

## Compare and award (contractor)

When the proposals arrive (or the deadline passes), open **Compare bids**: each proposal by stage, the total and the execution time, side by side.

![Compare the proposals](img:bids-comparar)

To choose, tap **Award** on the winning proposal and, if you wish, write a note.

![Award the bid](img:bids-fechar)

The other companies are marked **Not awarded**: they see the contractor went with another company, **without the winner's price**.

## The result for the winner

The winner sees **You were awarded this bid.** Their project becomes **Won** and the job is **linked to the contractor's job**, with nothing new to type.

![Result for the winning subcontractor](img:bids-venceu)
