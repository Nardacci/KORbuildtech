# On the iPhone (and Android)

KORbuild works like an app, with no app store: you install it from the browser.

## Install on the iPhone

1. Open the KORbuild address in **Safari** (other browsers cannot install the app on the iPhone).
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow, on the bottom bar).
3. Scroll and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Always open it from the **icon** on the Home Screen, not from Safari.

On Android, open it in Chrome and use **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** in the browser menu.

## Updates

The installed app updates itself. When there is a new version, a bar at the top tells you: **Downloading the new version** and **Updating the app**, and the page reloads. If you are in the middle of a form, the bar offers an **Update now** button, so you choose the moment.

The version in use appears at the bottom of the sign-in screen (for example, "KORbuild v164"). If the app does not update, close it by swiping up and open it again.

## Without signal

Daily keeps what you fill in on the device and sends it when the internet is back. Open the job and fill in the report as usual; when the signal returns, sending happens by itself.

## Sign out

Account menu (your initials) › **Sign out**. This ends the session on this device.
