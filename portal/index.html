KORbuild product guide

Everything KORbuild does, in one place.

KORbuild is a platform for construction companies in the United States: general contractors and the trade contractors they hire. This guide explains each module — what it is for, the rules it enforces, who can do what — and walks through every task with screenshots, so anyone can present the product with confidence.

Modules

One account, one set of jobs and people, shared by every module. Each company turns on the modules it needs.

DDailyDaily construction reports from the field: crew, weather, work performed, GPS-stamped photos and issues. The office reviews, approves and shares a sealed PDF with the client. Also home to the job list and the job schedule.CCrewCrew time clock with GPS and geofence, weekly timesheets with overtime, approval and payroll export, and labor cost by job: budget × actual × forecast to completion.MMeasurePlan takeoff in feet and inches: scale, measure, assemblies, quantities, quote lists and proposals.SSettingsCompany setup: employees, labor burden, work rules, jobs and their schedules, contacts, users and access roles, audit log.BBidsInvitations to bid between companies: the contracting company sends the bid package, levels the bids and awards; the subcontractor bids from Measure or by a link; a channel for questions that becomes the job conversation; subcontracting down the chain.
