# Roles and permissions

What each person can do in Bids comes from their **access role** (Settings › Access roles), like everything else in KORbuild.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Run bids and award** | Create and send bids, extend, invite more, answer questions, level, award and cancel; sees the bid amounts. |
| **Respond to invitations and submit bids** | See invitations, *I'll bid* or *Decline*, ask questions and submit bids through the platform. Includes *Measure plans*. |

## Default roles

| Role | Run bids | Respond to invitations |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Admin** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Project manager** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Foreman** and **Worker** | — | — |

!!! note "Good to know"
    - **Dollar amounts stay with the parties.** The leveling is only for who has *Run bids and award*.
    - **Responding needs Measure Takeoff.** A company without it answers invitations through the link, like a company without an account.
    - Typing the address of a Bids screen does not get around the permissions.
