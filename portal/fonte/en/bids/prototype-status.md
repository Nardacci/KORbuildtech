# Prototype status

What is simulated in the prototype today, and what comes next.

## Simulated today

| Item | In the prototype | In the product |
|---|---|---|
| Where bids live | In the browser, in a place shared by the demo companies. | The server, with each company reading only what it may see. |
| Invitations by email | Not sent. The contracting company uses **Copy the link** for invitees without an account. | Sent by email, with the private link. |
| Drawings | Only the PDF file names are kept; the invited project starts without sheets. | The PDFs go with the invitation and become the sheets of the invited company's project. |
| Bid PDF | Only the file name is kept. | Stored with the bid, downloadable by the contracting company. |
| Budget by cost code | Exists in the engine; the form takes only the total. | A budget field per cost code. |
| *Use in my estimate* | The sub's price goes into the proposal as **other items**, with your markups. | A subcontractor line in the catalog, by cost code. |
| Attachments in the channel | Text only. | Photos and PDFs. |

## Coming next

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sealed bids | The contracting company sees the amounts only after the due date (common in public work). |
| Change orders from the channel | A message becomes a formal change order. |
| Splitting the award | Award different cost codes to different subcontractors. |
| Inviting by email only | Invite someone who is not in Contacts yet. |
| Account from the link | When the subcontractor creates its account with the invitation email, the invitation, messages and bids move to it. |
