# Roles and permissions

What each person can do in Bids comes from their **access role** (Settings › Roles), like everything else in KORbuild.

## Permissions

| Permission | Group | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| **Run bids and award** | Daily | Create and send bids, extend, invite more, answer questions, level, award and cancel; sees the bid amounts. |
| **Respond to invitations and submit bids** | Measure | See invitations, *I'll bid* or *Decline*, ask questions and submit bids through the platform. Includes *Measure plans*. |

The job channel after the award has no permission of its own in the prototype: it shows on the job page, to whoever already opens the job (*View all jobs*).

## Default roles

| Role | Run bids | Respond to invitations | Notes |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Admin** | ✓ | ✓ | |
| **Project manager** | ✓ | ✓ | |
| **Foreman** | — | — | Does not see Bids. |
| **Worker** | — | — | Does not see Bids. |

!!! note "Good to know"
    - **Responding needs Measure in the plan.** A subcontractor without Measure answers invitations through the link, like a company without an account.
    - **Dollar amounts stay with the parties.** The general contractor's management view (crew on site) never shows bid amounts; the leveling is only for who has *Run bids and award*.
    - Typing the address of a Bids screen does not get around the permissions.
