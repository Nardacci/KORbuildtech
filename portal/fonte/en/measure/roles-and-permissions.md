# Roles and permissions

What each person can do in Measure comes from their **access role** (Settings › Access roles). The company can adjust the default roles or create new ones.

## Measure permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Measure plans** | Open Measure: projects, upload PDFs, set the scale, measure, add and apply assemblies, see quantities and materials, material lists for quotes and proposals. |
| **Items and assemblies** | Create and edit items, assemblies with formulas, prices and markups. Includes **Measure plans**. Without it, formulas and prices are read-only. |

## Default roles

| Role | Measure | Items and assemblies | Notes |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Admin** | ✓ | ✓ | Usually the owner or the chief estimator. |
| **Project manager** | ✓ | — | Measures and prices, but does not change the catalog. |
| **Foreman** | — | — | Does not see Measure. |
| **Worker** | — | — | Does not see Measure. |

!!! note "Good to know"
    - Measure is offered to companies that **do the work** (subcontractors, or companies that both hire and do the work). A general contractor does not have Measure: it receives the proposal by email or PDF.
    - Suppliers and clients are **contacts**, not users: they receive the quote request or the proposal and need no account.
    - Typing the address of a Measure screen does not get around the permissions.
